# StudyCircle
Learn together. Grow together.

An 18+ student collaboration platform for reciprocal academic help, communities and private study tools. The classroom journey begins with a subject match, continues in an accepted conversation and ends with a mutually confirmed session and eligible feedback. Other journeys include anonymous Q&A, shared study rooms and reporting. Public surfaces use aliases by default. Participants choose identifiable profile details and can opt into coarse discovery. Success is measured through actual stored activity.
